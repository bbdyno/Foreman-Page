Privacy Policy

Foreman has no servers run by its developer, and the developer does not collect your personal information.

Summary

The data Foreman works with is stored only on your devices and in your iCloud. The developer cannot receive or see it.

  • There is no sign-up. We don’t ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • No advertising identifiers, analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • Session content travels between your devices end-to-end encrypted.

Information the developer collects

None. The developer runs no relay, logging or account servers, so none of your information is sent to the developer.

Data the app handles and where it is stored

The app needs the data below to work. All of it is stored only in places you control.

  • Session output and history (an agent’s terminal output, conversation and changes): on the Mac where the agent runs. When your devices can’t connect directly, it passes through the CloudKit private database in your iCloud.
  • Approval requests and questions: on your Mac, your devices and the CloudKit private database in your iCloud.
  • Pairing details and API keys: in the keychain of your devices. Agent API keys stay in the keychain of the Mac where the agent runs.
  • Settings and approval rules: on your devices and in your iCloud.

Data stored in iCloud is subject to Apple’s iCloud terms and privacy policy.

Communication with other services

  • Apple: the app uses iCloud (CloudKit), push notifications and App Store payments. Notifications are sent by Apple directly, through a subscription registered in your iCloud.
  • Public STUN servers (stun.cloudflare.com, stun.l.google.com): used only to learn your public IP address while finding a direct route between your devices. No session content is sent. Each operator’s own policy applies.
  • Agent providers: agents such as Claude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI run on your Mac because you start them, and they talk to their own services under their providers’ terms and privacy policies. Foreman does not intercept their traffic or send data on their behalf.

Crash reports and diagnostics

The app contains no crash-reporting tool of its own. The only crash information the developer receives is what each store (App Store, Google Play, Microsoft Store) provides by default, and only if you agreed in your device settings to share it with developers. It does not identify you.

Payments

Subscriptions are processed by the App Store. The developer receives no payment or billing details. The Mac app checks your subscription by reading the App Store–signed record from your iCloud.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your devices and in your iCloud until you delete it. On the Free plan, session history older than 7 days is deleted automatically. Deleting the app removes its data from that device; data left in iCloud can be removed on iPhone: open Settings, tap your name, then iCloud and Manage Account Storage, and choose Foreman. Session history on your Mac is deleted from the Mac app’s settings.

This website

This website is served by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no external font services. GitHub may process access logs (such as IP addresses) to operate its service, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Children

Foreman is a tool for software developers and is not directed at children under 13. The developer collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new text and its effective date will be posted on this page. You can see every change in the GitHub repository.

Contact

For questions about privacy, open an issue on GitHub Issues. Please don’t put personal information in a public issue.

An app for managing the coding agents that run on your computer from iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, Android and Windows. It works without any servers of ours.
